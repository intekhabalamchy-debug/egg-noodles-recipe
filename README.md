# 🍜 Quick Egg & Noodles (ポニョ風熱々たまご麺)

> *"Savory Stir-Fried Egg Noodles with Crisp Garden Vegetables & Shoyu Umami — Straight out of a cozy Ghibli kitchen scene."*

A responsive culinary showcase website inspired by Studio Ghibli comfort food scenes—evoking the late-night storm noodles in *Ponyo* and Calcifer's sizzling skillet in *Howl's Moving Castle*.

---

## ⚡ Quick Recipe Specs

| Stat | Detail |
|---|---|
| **⏱️ Prep Time** | 5 Minutes |
| **🍳 Cook Time** | 10 Minutes |
| **🥣 Servings** | 2 Bowls |
| **⭐ Skill Level** | Easy |

---

## ✨ Features

- **🎨 Studio Ghibli Aesthetic:** Soft floating ambient glow, terracotta & sea color palette, and custom typography (*Noto Serif JP*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*).
- **🥘 Split-Screen Hero:** Highlighting dish presentation alongside quick cooking chips and a *Ghibli Kitchen Secret* tip box.
- **📋 Interactive Ingredients Checklist:** Clean pantry breakdown with custom status indicators and quantities.
- **🔢 6-Step Visual Timeline:** Structured preparation guide from boiling noodles and wok-searing veggies to scrambling velvety eggs and shoyu glazing.
- **📱 Fully Responsive Design:** Handcrafted with CSS Grid & Flexbox, adapting seamlessly to mobile, tablet, and widescreen viewports.

---

## 🥢 Ingredients Overview

- 1 cup dry egg or ramen noodles
- 2 tbsp toasted sesame or vegetable oil
- 1 cup mixed garden vegetables (peas, carrots, peppers)
- 1 large farm-fresh egg
- 1 tbsp Japanese shoyu (soy sauce)
- Sea salt & crushed black pepper to taste
